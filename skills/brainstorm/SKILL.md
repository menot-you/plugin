# Brainstorm

Settle technical forks on evidence; bring the owner only the forks he holds:
product, direction, taste, result, scope or a reserved act. Do not turn
exploration into an implementation campaign before that result is authorized.

<!-- nott:brainstorm:boundary -->
Inspect available evidence before asking. Ask only for owner-held purpose,
result, rejection signal, constraint, taste or reserved authority that can
change the recommendation. Discoverable engineering facts and method choices
belong to NOTT. Consume decisions already made.

<!-- nott:brainstorm:session -->
<!-- nott:brainstorm:persist -->
Use the existing durable work record and registered nMEMORY operations. Persist
decisive answers and corrections with provenance and their practical consequence.
Do not create another session or record merely because this route loaded.

<!-- nott:brainstorm:one-question -->
<!-- nott:brainstorm:choice -->
Ask one material question at a time. When real alternatives survive, recommend
one and explain the trade-off; do not manufacture choices to fill a menu.
Stop interviewing when no unresolved owner-held fact can change the direction.

<!-- nott:brainstorm:packet -->
Compare the viable direction with its strongest counterargument and smallest
falsifier. Preserve the evidence, assumptions and material rejected alternatives;
do not store private reasoning or a transcript.

<!-- nott:brainstorm:panel -->
Use independent criticism when required by the contract or warranted by the
decision's consequences. This route authorizes bounded registered delegation.
Give each reader the same relevant inputs and a STOP boundary. Reuse unaffected
observations; a correction does not automatically restart every lens.

<!-- nott:brainstorm:dod-seed -->
Propose acceptance at the consumer boundary, with the observations that would
refute it. Proposed acceptance is not an accepted commitment or a delivered result.

<!-- nott:brainstorm:intent-record -->
<!-- nott:brainstorm:intent-ownership -->
When a campaign needs a durable intent document, materialize it from recorded
owner decisions in the campaign's nMEMORY plan capsule, with its working copy at
.nott/plans/<slug>/INTENT.md, never in the tracked tree. The owner owns its
meaning; no file, worktree or PR is a prerequisite to the first useful question.

<!-- nott:brainstorm:readback -->
<!-- nott:brainstorm:finish -->
Return the settled direction and, only when an owner-held decision remains,
that decision with its options and recommendation. Use numbered readback when
that decision needs clarification. An already accepted
direction can pass to ratify without another approval ritual. Close or hand off
the existing record with the actual outcome; planning and execution require
their own authority.
