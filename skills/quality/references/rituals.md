# What does not mechanize

Seven properties are held by a recorded human round. Each lives as a ritual row
naming a cadence and an owner, never as a fake number. A round records its date
and its findings in the repository; an overdue cadence is stale rather than
absent.

## Q11 — naming judgment

Cadence: every candidate, at review. Owner: the reviewer of record. The grep for
`utils`, `helpers`, `manager`, `data` and `misc` is a negative proxy and is never
reported as a score. A match becomes a finding only when the reviewer names the
decision or responsibility the name obscures, and the finding then says what the
module does; otherwise the match is dropped.

## Q13 — the wrong abstraction

Cadence: every candidate that removes duplication. Owner: the author, checked by
the reviewer. A dedupe must name the shared decision it removes; a dedupe that
can only name shared text is refused, because the wrong abstraction invented to
dry up two things that only looked alike costs more than the duplication did.

## Q09 — deletability

Cadence: quarterly deletion drill. Owner: the maintainer on rotation. Remove one
feature on a throwaway branch and record every file that bleeds. The bleeding
file list is the finding, and it is a design finding.

## Q14 — YAGNI

Cadence: quarterly speculation review. Owner: the maintainer on rotation. A
single-implementation seam must justify itself or be deleted. The harvest of
deliberate-simplification markers is this ritual's method: every marker is one
row, and a marker naming no upgrade trigger is a finding.

## Q02 — sabotage

Cadence: every enrolled suite, each round. Owner: the author of the guarded
behavior. Break a guarded behavior on purpose and observe the suite turn red. A
test never seen red is not proof. Assertion inversion is never that proof.

## Q04 — the cold-reader drill

Cadence: quarterly, and on any structural move. Owner: the maintainer on
rotation. A reader with no guide and no tribal memory recovers the declared
architecture from the directory tree alone, or the tree is the finding.

## Q06 — history sampling

Cadence: monthly, over a fixed sample size. Owner: the maintainer on rotation.
Score commit-message intent and diff cohesion over the sample. The sampled rate
must not fall between rounds; sampling is what makes this affordable at all.
