# Progress and stopping

Keep the accepted result and its integrated consumer judge as the identity of
the work. Maintain cumulative attempt history under that identity across
commits, reviews, subtasks, symptoms, workers, sessions and resumptions.
Renaming or moving the work never resets misses or spending.

Before another attempt, state internally which delivery decision it can change,
the causal hypothesis, the one changed input, the deciding observation and its
limit. After two misses without differentiating evidence, suspend that path. A
miss in the proof's own scaffolding (harness, fixture, wait, lock or capture)
counts against that path whatever its cause; a new scaffolding cause is not
differentiating evidence about the result, so after two such misses prove the
scaffolding offline against the last run's captured evidence before spending
another consumer slot. Preserve a reduced reproducer and both failures. Resume
only when new evidence addresses the cause and supports a bounded next attempt;
retain the history. More logs, another prompt wording or an occasional pass do
not establish a fix.

Distinguish plugin/product defects, evaluator or fixture defects, client
limitations, environment failures, operational failures and uncertain effects.
Reconcile an uncertain effect before retrying. A prerequisite repair inherits
neither the result's scope nor permission to run indefinitely.

Expose progress in terms of the requested outcome: implemented, verified
locally, observed at the consumer, qualified. Do not turn completed subtasks,
lines, elapsed time or test counts into a percentage of delivery, and never
invent a duration: when how long it will take is unknown, name the next
observable event and who emits it. During long work the state line carries
progress; the screen gets only a new blocker. When no authorized action can change the decision, give the
exact blocker and a concrete recommendation.
